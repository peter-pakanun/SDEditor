# SDEditor (Discord Post Helper)

## Short Post (copy/paste)

**SDEditor** — browser-based editor for `StatDescriptions.zip` (translation stat files).

- [Click here to open the live web app](https://sdeditor.pages.dev/)
- [Click here to open the GitHub repo](https://github.com/peter-pakanun/SDEditor)

**How to use**
1) Open the web app and pick your target language
2) Drag & drop `StatDescriptions.zip` into the page
3) Click any row to edit, then save
4) Click 💾 to export `StatDescriptions_Translated.zip` (Ctrl+Click 💾 = full export)

**Notes**
- Your in-progress edits are stored in your browser (`localStorage`)
- You can drag & drop a previously exported ZIP onto the table to re-import edits
